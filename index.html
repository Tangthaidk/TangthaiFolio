Bridging research and people

My work sits at the intersection of design and communication — translating complex information into visuals that people can act on. From medical tele-health in Paris to anti-trafficking campaigns in Southeast Asia, I've built materials that meet audiences where they are.

I bring experience across the full production spectrum: from developing brand standards and designing publications to editing video and building WordPress sites. I've applied branding frameworks at every organization I've worked with.

My background in interdisciplinary research (MSc, Université Paris Cité) shapes how I approach briefs — with curiosity about systems and a commitment to clarity over decoration.
