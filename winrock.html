Overview

Design for every audience

Designed social behavior change communication materials for USAID's Counter-Trafficking in Persons program, reaching migrant communities across Southeast Asia. Visual language had to work across cultural contexts and low-literacy audiences.

As Digital Content Coordinator, my focus was social behaviour change communication (SBCC), creating campaign visuals and social media content for the Inside Outsider and MAYDAY campaigns.

  • Campaign visuals
  • Social media content
  • Audience-adapted design
